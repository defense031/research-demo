01 · The agent

An agent takes steps toward your goal

Think of a teammate who already has your project open. You bring the intent and the direction; it handles the execution, right in your files.

  • Reads your files to gather its own context
  • Runs your code and debugs it to meet your intent
  • Fetches: downloads, searches, web APIs
  • Touches only what you permit
  • Can loop until the job is done

02 · Sifting the literature

How to run an agentic literature review

This automates the worst part of a literature review: the endless search through papers that might fit your inclusion criteria but turn out to be no practical use to you.

one command · four subagents in parallel · a tracker and the raw files

Give it to the agents
  • Search, retrieve, deduplicate
  • Screen abstracts against your criteria
  • Extract methods and results into one table
Still your responsibility
  • Article content, and what you cite it for
  • Is the gap real, or did your search miss it?
  • Page numbers and sections for every citation

06 · Version control

    
Push a project into its own folder on GitHub

    
This uses the same skill pattern as Overleaf. A map names the files
    that leave the laptop and the repo folder they land in. Everything else stays local.

    

      

        

          
commit → /github_push

          

        

        
        
Git keeps every version, and the map keeps data and secrets on your laptop.

The so what
